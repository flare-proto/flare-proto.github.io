## 2. What this does to people

for the adults that care about privacy this takes spaces away from them

for the kids, it isolates them, makes life less enjoyable, hurts their ability to become functioning members of society in the future.

### 2.1. Why cant kids have a space

Malls are too expensive, you ***might*** get an hour at school, parents dont want to constantly have that many people around, people dont like seeing groups of teens lingering around, so the city parks are policed and gas is expensive so further parks are prohibitive too, theres no crown land near the cities either, you cant be noisy in a library.
So where do the kids go? they rely on online spaces for a legal space to hang out.

what happens to the kids when the spaces they use become illegal too? they arent just going to stop hanging out, they just will stop caring if where they spend their time is illegal.
